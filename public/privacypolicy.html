Tally privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026. Tally is a personal nutrition and fitness app run by Steve Schaller for himself, friends and family. Contact: schallerrsteve@gmail.com.

What Tally stores

Your account email, the foods, workouts, weight, water and targets you log, and the people, messages, achievements and challenges you take part in. This is stored in a Supabase database (hosted in Canada) and on your own device so the app works offline.

Health Connect and watch data

If you choose to connect, Tally reads steps, exercise sessions, calories burned and weight from Health Connect on your Android phone. Tally uses this data only to show your activity and add workouts and weight to your own diary. It is stored privately in your account, is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone. Friends never see your Health Connect data. Tally does not write anything to Health Connect. You can stop syncing in Tally's Profile screen and remove Tally's access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Who can see your data

Only you can see your food diary, workouts and health data. Friends you accept can see your name, the achievements you choose to post, and the summary stats you switch on in Friends → People (for example workouts this week). Database rules enforce this.

Other services

Barcode lookups send the barcode number to Open Food Facts. If enabled, photos of nutrition labels you choose to scan are sent to Anthropic's Claude to read the numbers; they are not stored.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete my account permanently removes your account and everything in it.
